A 100-word monologue spoken by a fantasy villain who has ruled the Ashen
Spires for three centuries — and is, quietly, running on empty. The
tiredness lives in the pauses, the stairs, the ravens' complaint about the
hours, and a chair brought in for the last hero. He never says he is tired.
He offers the hero tea instead.

`main.ts` writes the monologue to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`.
Self-contained, Node 22 stdlib only, no network, no files read.
